About Me

My main interests are offensive security, vulnerability research, exploit development, reverse engineering and low-level systems. I enjoy taking software apart, understanding its internals and turning bugs into a deeper understanding of the underlying system.

Alongside research, I build security tooling and document what I learn through technical writeups and vulnerability research.

If you have any questions about my blog, feel free to reach out. If you have interesting research in linux userland / kernel VR, I’m down for anything as long as it’s remote or in South Africa. (my resume).

  • I do CTFs for Dead Pirate Society, and work on challenges in pwn and kernel exploitation.
  • Currently, I’m an Information Technology student at Richfield.
